# Research notes — 17 November 2026

Researched on 18 November 2026 (WITA) via news search. Only stories
verified as happening, or first reported, on Tuesday 17 November 2026
were used. Figures and quotes below are as reported; nothing was
invented.

## Main stories (poster cards)

1. **The Gulf — US strikes Iran's Qeshm Island.** US jets struck an
   Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island, described by US officials as
   self-defence, after Iran fired drones at a US radar at Sheikh Isa
   base in Bahrain — Iran's third attack on Bahrain in five days.
   Sources: One Turn daily brief (citing CBS News, NPR, Al Jazeera),
   Argus US brief, Standard Topic world archive.
2. **Ukraine — Kerch Bridge span falls.** Ukrainian drones and missiles
   struck the Kerch Bridge to Crimea for a second day; a road section
   fell into the strait. President Zelenskyy called the operation
   "unprecedented". Sources: BBC, One Turn, GlobalsConflicts
   (France 24, NPR, DW, Guardian).
3. **Asia summit — Trump in Manila, headed for Xi–Putin talks.** Trump
   arrived in Manila on 17 November for ASEAN engagements and the first
   Quad leaders' summit since September 2024, announcing
   critical-minerals agreements; a trilateral meeting with Xi Jinping
   and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen (APEC) was planned later in the week.
   Sources: Argus US brief, FeedCast daily brief, BBC.
4. **Tech — Nvidia record results, Huang stays out.** Nvidia reported
   third-quarter revenue of $115.8 billion, about double a year
   earlier, and said it would not reinstate ousted CEO Jensen Huang.
   Sources: BBC, Argus US brief, One Turn.
5. **Mongolia — president says he will resign.** President Ukhnaagiin
   Khurelsukh announced on 17 November that he would resign over
   railway-contract bribery allegations, denying wrongdoing and saying
   he would cooperate with investigators; the resignation takes effect
   only once parliament (State Great Khural) accepts it. Sources: AP
   reporting via AnewZ, KT.kz, World Pulse Wire, People Daily Digital.
6. **Health — US loses measles-free status.** The US lost its measles
   elimination status after 26 years amid record case numbers, with
   states reporting over 5,400 cases and 10 measles-related deaths.
   Source: BBC.

## Ticker stories

- **Ebola in DR Congo passes 11,000 cases**, with over 5,500 deaths
  since the outbreak was declared on 15 May 2026; Reuters reported on
  17 November that it was spreading at a record pace.
- **Russian An-12 military transport crash in Siberia** (Kemerovo
  region) killed all 19 aboard, Russia's defence ministry said, citing
  a preliminary technical malfunction. (Some early reports said 17;
  the poster follows the ministry figure as reported by Reuters/BBC.)
- **Five EU states plan a Rwanda return hub**: Germany, Denmark,
  Austria, Greece and the Netherlands announced a joint facility in
  Rwanda for rejected asylum seekers, with a pilot planned for 2027.
  Sources: FeedCast, Europa Express News.
- **Japan scrambles jets** after two suspected Russian aircraft
  violated its airspace; Japan lodged a strong protest. Sources:
  Asas Press world digest, FeedCast.
